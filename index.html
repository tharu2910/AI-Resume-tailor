Check your resume against a job posting before you apply

Paste a job posting and your resume. The site explains what the job asks for, shows which skills your resume is missing, and gives you five interview questions to practise for those gaps.

Why we made this

We surveyed 27 college students, mostly second-years. Their answers pointed to one problem: they do not know what employers expect.

59%said knowing what companies expect is the hardest part of a resume
93%find job descriptions hard to understand at least sometimes
78%worry their resume will not be shortlisted

How it works

  1. Log in with the demo login and open your dashboard.
  2. On the Analyze page, paste a job posting and your resume.
  3. The Results page lists each requirement in plain words and marks what is missing from your resume.
  4. The Interview prep page gives five questions with talking points built from your gaps.

The site checks about 60 common skills by matching words, so it can miss a skill written in other words. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
