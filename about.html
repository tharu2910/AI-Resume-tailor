About the project

This site is the prototype from our Design Thinking project on AI-Powered Resume Tailor for Grads. This page summarises the research behind it.

Survey results

We asked 27 students (25 second-year, 2 first-year) in October 2026. Because almost everyone is early in college, the results describe early-year students, not final-year job seekers.

QuestionResult
Hardest part of a resumeKnowing what companies expect: 16 students (59%). Making it look good: 4 (15%). Writing project details: 3 (11%).
Job descriptions hard to understandSometimes 21 (78%), yes 4 (15%), no 2 (7%)
Worried about being shortlistedYes 7 (26%), sometimes 14 (52%)

Most helpful feature (pick any)Improve my resume 59%, improve project descriptions 44%, find missing skills 26%, show resume-job match 26%, check formatting 22%, find keywords 19%

The problem we chose

Early-year college students need a clear way to understand what a job description expects and how their resume compares. Most find job descriptions hard to read, and most worry about being shortlisted.

Design Thinking steps

  1. Empathize. Survey, persona, empathy map and journey map.
  2. Define. One problem statement and four How Might We questions.
  3. Ideate. Brainstorming, Six Thinking Hats and assumption testing.
  4. Prototype. This website: a demo login, a dashboard, the Analyze page, a success screen with Results, and Interview prep. A mock API layer sits between the pages and the matching engine.
  5. Test. Planned: students try the site and we fix what confuses them.

Similar tools that already exist

Other tools already generate interview questions from a job description and resume, so that feature alone is not new. What we want to test is whether early-year students find plain-language requirements and gap-based talking points more useful.

ToolWhat it does
Weekday Interview Question PredictorFree. Takes a job description and resume and gives 10 likely questions with sample answers.
AI ResumeGuru mock interviewAsks questions one at a time and targets requirements the resume does not show. Free tier allows one full interview every 30 days.
Jobscan, Teal, Rezi and othersMatch resumes to job descriptions and track applications.

Limits of this prototype

  • It looks for about 60 common skills by matching words. It does not understand meaning.
  • It uses no AI model. The talking points are templates filled in from your gaps.
  • It has not been tested with students yet.
